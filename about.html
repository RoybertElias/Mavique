About Mavique

Learn what Mavique publishes and how the site approaches software and AI guidance.

What Mavique is

Mavique is an independent editorial site focused on practical AI, business software, productivity and workflow automation. The goal is to help small-business owners understand tools before they spend time or money adopting them.

How we approach recommendations

We focus on use cases, workflow fit, limitations and trade-offs rather than simply listing features. Product capabilities, prices and availability can change, so readers should verify current details with the provider.

Editorial independence

Mavique aims to keep editorial decisions separate from commercial incentives. If the site uses affiliate links or other commercial relationships, those relationships are disclosed on the Affiliate Disclosure page.

Corrections

If you notice an outdated or inaccurate statement, please use the site owner’s published contact channel so the information can be reviewed and corrected.
