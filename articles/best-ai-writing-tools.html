Best AI Writing Tools

How to evaluate AI writing tools for drafting, editing and content workflows.

Editorial guide • Updated October 2026

Choose the job

Decide whether you need brainstorming, first drafts, editing, summarization or style transformation. Different workflows require different evaluation criteria.

Check the editing burden

A fast first draft is only useful if the final review remains manageable. Test representative writing and measure how much correction is needed.

Protect sensitive information

Review data controls before using confidential customer, employee or business information. Use approved workflows for sensitive material.

Bottom line

Choose the writing assistant that improves your process while preserving human review and your own editorial standards.

Editorial note: Product features, prices, availability and policies can change. Verify current details with the provider before making a purchase or business decision.
