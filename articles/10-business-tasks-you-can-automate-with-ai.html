10 Business Tasks You Can Automate With AI

Ten practical categories of repetitive work that can be candidates for AI-assisted automation.

Editorial guide • Updated October 2026

Good candidates

Email triage, meeting summaries, first-draft documents, FAQ responses, content repurposing, lead qualification, data classification, internal reporting, task creation and research summaries can all be candidates.

Start small

Pick one task, define the input and output, add a review checkpoint and measure the result.

Know what not to automate

High-risk decisions, sensitive data handling and work that requires professional judgment may need stronger controls or should remain human-led.

Bottom line

Automation works best when the process is already clear and the human owner remains accountable.
