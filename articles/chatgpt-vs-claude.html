ChatGPT vs Claude

A practical comparison framework for businesses choosing a general-purpose AI assistant.

Editorial guide • Updated October 2026

Compare workflows, not headlines

General-purpose AI assistants can overlap heavily, so the better question is which one fits your actual work. Test the same representative tasks in each system rather than relying only on feature lists.

Use examples from your business: summarizing a long document, drafting a customer response, brainstorming campaign ideas, extracting action items, or helping with a spreadsheet workflow.

Quality and reliability

Evaluate whether answers are accurate, clear and consistent for your use case. Check important outputs yourself, especially when the result affects money, customers, compliance or other high-consequence decisions.

Keep a small evaluation set of recurring tasks. That makes future model or product changes easier to assess.

Privacy and workflow fit

Review the provider's current data controls, account options and terms before entering sensitive information. Then consider integrations, collaboration features and how easily the tool fits into the team's existing habits.

Bottom line

There is no universal winner. Pick the assistant that performs best on the tasks you actually do and that fits your organization's privacy and workflow requirements.

Editorial note: Product features, prices, availability and policies can change. Verify current details with the provider before making a purchase or business decision.
