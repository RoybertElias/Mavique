HubSpot vs Salesforce

A framework for comparing two well-known CRM approaches without choosing based on brand recognition alone.

Editorial guide • Updated October 2026

Start with complexity

A growing business should first decide how complex its sales, marketing and service processes really are. More capability can be useful, but it also creates more configuration and administration.

Document the workflows you need today and the workflows you realistically expect within the next stage of growth.

Compare the operating model

Evaluate contact and opportunity management, automation, reporting, integrations, permissions and customization. Ask which capabilities your team will use every week rather than which ones exist on a feature page.

Implementation effort matters too. Migration, data cleanup, training and process design can be more significant than the software license itself.

Make the decision measurable

Create a scorecard with categories such as usability, required features, integrations, reporting, administration and total cost. Weight the categories according to your business priorities.

Bottom line

The right CRM is the platform that supports your process at an acceptable level of complexity and cost. Revisit the decision as your process changes.

Editorial note: Product features, prices, availability and policies can change. Verify current details with the provider before making a purchase or business decision.
