How to Measure AI ROI

A simple framework for estimating whether an AI workflow is creating enough value to justify its cost.

Editorial guide • Updated October 2026

Start with a baseline

Measure how long the existing task takes, how often it happens and how many people touch it. Without a baseline, it is difficult to know whether the new workflow actually improved anything.

Calculate time value

Estimate hours saved over a month and multiply by a reasonable internal value for that time. Be conservative: time that is technically saved but immediately replaced by other work is not necessarily cash savings.

Include quality and risk

Record how often the AI output needs correction and whether errors create additional cost. For customer-facing or sensitive work, include review time and the potential cost of mistakes.

Review the full cost

Include subscriptions, implementation, integrations, training and ongoing administration. Compare that total with the measured benefit over the same period.

Bottom line

AI ROI is a measurement exercise, not a promise. Keep the calculation simple, use real data and revisit it as the workflow changes.

Editorial note: Product features, prices, availability and policies can change. Verify current details with the provider before making a purchase or business decision.
