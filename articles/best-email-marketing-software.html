Best Email Marketing Software

What to compare when choosing email marketing software for a small business.

Editorial guide • Updated October 2026

Define the audience

Know whether you need newsletters, automated sequences, transactional messages or a combination.

Compare core workflow

Evaluate list management, segmentation, templates, automation, analytics and integrations.

Respect consent

Build permission and unsubscribe handling into your process and follow the laws that apply to your audience.

Bottom line

The best email platform is one that supports your audience strategy and keeps list management simple.

Editorial note: Product features, prices, availability and policies can change. Verify current details with the provider before making a purchase or business decision.
