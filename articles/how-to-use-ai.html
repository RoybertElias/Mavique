How to Use AI to Automate Your Small Business

A step-by-step framework for finding repetitive work that can be improved with AI while keeping people in control.

Editorial guide • Updated October 2026

1. Find repetitive work

Start with tasks that happen often and follow a recognizable pattern: drafting replies, summarizing notes, classifying inquiries, creating first drafts or preparing internal updates.

Avoid automating a task simply because it is possible. Prioritize work where the current process is slow and the cost of mistakes is manageable.

2. Define the human checkpoint

Decide exactly where a person reviews the output. AI-generated work should have an owner, especially when the result is customer-facing, financial, legal or otherwise consequential.

3. Standardize the inputs

Automation works better when the input is predictable. Create a short template or checklist describing what information the workflow should receive and what the output should contain.

4. Measure the result

Track time saved, correction rate and the quality of the final output. Compare the new process with the old one instead of assuming automation is automatically an improvement.

Bottom line

Use AI to remove repetitive steps, not accountability. A small workflow that is reliable and measurable is a better starting point than a large automation project.
