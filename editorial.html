Editorial Policy

How Mavique researches, evaluates and updates editorial content.

Purpose

Mavique publishes practical guides intended to help readers make informed decisions about AI tools, software and business workflows.

Research and verification

Articles are written to explain use cases and trade-offs. Product-specific claims should be checked against current first-party documentation before publication or updating. We avoid presenting temporary prices or feature availability as permanent facts.

Originality

Mavique content is written for this site and is intended to add explanation, comparison or practical context rather than reproduce provider marketing copy.

Updates and corrections

Technology changes quickly. Articles may be revised when important product capabilities, pricing, policies or recommendations change. Readers should verify current information before making a purchase or operational decision.

AI-assisted work

AI tools may be used as part of research or drafting workflows, but published material is reviewed and edited for clarity, accuracy and usefulness. Human editorial responsibility remains with the site owner.
